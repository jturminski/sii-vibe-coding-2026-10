# Return Terms (Regulamin zwrotów) — EXAMPLE

> Illustrative example for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot MVP. Based on the Polish Consumer Rights Act (Ustawa o prawach konsumenta). Must be reviewed by legal before production use.

## §1 Scope
1. These terms apply to consumers who bought equipment from the Store online (distance contract).
2. The purchase date stated by the customer is treated as the delivery date.

## §2 Right of withdrawal
1. The customer may withdraw from the contract without giving a reason within **14 days** of delivery.
2. The deadline is met if the withdrawal is submitted before it expires.
3. A return submitted later than 14 days after delivery is not accepted under these terms.

## §3 Condition of returned equipment
1. The customer may handle the equipment only as needed to establish its nature, characteristics and functioning (as in a physical store).
2. Equipment showing **no signs of use** beyond §3 ust. 1 and no damage is accepted for return.
3. If the equipment shows signs of use beyond §3 ust. 1 (scratches, wear, dirt, missing protective films with marks of use), the right of withdrawal **is not lost**, but the customer is liable for the reduced value. Such cases are referred to a specialist, who determines the reduction.
4. If the equipment is damaged, the case is referred to a specialist.

## §4 Exclusions
1. The right of withdrawal does not apply to sealed goods that cannot be returned for health or hygiene reasons once unsealed after delivery. In particular, this includes **in-ear headphones/earbuds** with a broken hygienic seal.
2. Equipment made to the customer's specification is excluded.

## §5 Procedure
1. After a positive decision, the customer sends the equipment back within **14 days** of the withdrawal, preferably with all accessories and in the original packaging.
2. The customer bears the direct cost of returning the equipment.
3. The Store refunds the payment within **14 days** of receiving the withdrawal, using the original payment method. The Store may withhold the refund until it receives the equipment or proof of its dispatch.
4. The final decision is made after physical inspection of the equipment.
