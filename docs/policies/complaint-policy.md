# Complaint Terms (Regulamin reklamacji) — EXAMPLE

> Illustrative example for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot MVP. Based on the Polish Consumer Rights Act (Ustawa o prawach konsumenta), chapter 5a, non-conformity of goods with the contract. Must be reviewed by legal before production use.

## §1 Scope
1. These terms cover the Store's liability for non-conformity of equipment with the contract (statutory seller liability).
2. They do not cover a manufacturer warranty (gwarancja). If one exists, the customer may use it separately.
3. The purchase date stated by the customer is treated as the delivery date.

## §2 Liability period
1. The Store is liable for non-conformity that becomes apparent within **2 years** of delivery.
2. Non-conformity that becomes apparent within that period is presumed to have existed at delivery, unless the Store proves otherwise.
3. Complaints about equipment delivered more than 2 years ago are not accepted under these terms.

## §3 What counts as non-conformity
1. Non-conformity includes manufacturing and material defects, and faults appearing during normal use (for example, a failing screen, buttons, ports, a battery that does not hold charge contrary to its specification, or hinge or housing defects without signs of external force).
2. Non-conformity is **not** damage caused by the customer after delivery, in particular:
   - mechanical damage from impact or a fall (cracked screen or housing with an impact point, dents, bent frame),
   - liquid damage (corrosion, liquid indicators triggered),
   - damage from improper use, unauthorised repairs or modifications.
3. Normal wear consistent with the age and intended use of the equipment (for example, minor scratches) is not non-conformity, unless it affects functioning earlier than can reasonably be expected.

## §4 Assessment
1. When the cause cannot be determined from the description and the photo (for example, an internal fault not visible from outside, or conflicting signs), the complaint is referred to a specialist for inspection.
2. When the description and the photo contradict each other, the complaint is referred to a specialist.

## §5 Remedies
1. The customer may first request **repair or replacement**. The Store may choose the other option if the requested one is impossible or disproportionately costly.
2. **Price reduction or withdrawal from the contract** is available when repair or replacement is impossible, was refused, failed, or the non-conformity is significant.
3. Withdrawal is not available if the non-conformity is insignificant. Non-conformity is presumed significant.

## §6 Procedure
1. The Store responds to a complaint within **14 days** of receiving it.
2. After a positive preliminary decision, the customer sends the equipment to the Store's service centre. The Store covers the shipping cost for accepted complaints.
3. The final decision is made after physical inspection of the equipment.
